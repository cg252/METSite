Events & Programming

From weekly general meetings to recruiting kickoffs and firm nights — here's where MTT members show up.

General Meeting

Weekly General Meeting

Weekly · Time TBD each semester Room TBD, U-M Campus

Education Session

New Member Education

Weekly, first semester Immediately after General Meeting

A weekly curriculum for new members on both tracks — building technical skills early in their college careers.

Recruiting

Fall Recruiting Kickoff

Start of Fall semester Info session, both tracks

Interview Rounds

After application deadline Behavioral + track-specific technical

Development Session

Desk Work Session

Weekly, per desk Lab / hardware bench

Hands-on build time for desks in the Research Phase — strategy backtesting, RTL development, and system integration.

Firm Event

Industry Firm Night

Monthly, during the semester Partner/sponsor hosted

Talks and Q&As with trading firms, hardware companies, and alumni on recruiting and industry partnerships.

Social
