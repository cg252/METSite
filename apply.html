Apply to MET

Membership is earned through merit alone. Every applicant — regardless of connections — completes the same application and interview process.

Article IV · Section 2

The Process

STEP 01

Application

Apply at the start of the semester to either the Trading or Hardware track. Your track determines the rest of your process.

STEP 02

Interviews

Selected applicants interview with the board — behavioral questions plus technical content specific to your track.

STEP 03

Offer

Once all interview rounds conclude, the board extends membership to the top candidates in each track.

Pick One to Start

Trading Track or Hardware Track?

Both tracks work side-by-side on every desk — choose the one that matches where you want to start.

Trading Track

Research, Risk & Live Execution

You'll work alongside hardware and software members on a project desk — moving a strategy from research through live, risk-managed trading.

  • Market & strategy research, backtesting
  • Risk management & position limits
  • Technical interview on markets & quantitative reasoning
  • Works with employer trading-restriction policies in mind
Hardware Track

FPGAs, RTL & Low-Latency Systems

You'll design and build the hardware-accelerated systems that get a desk's strategy to the exchange — faster.

  • FPGA & RTL development (Verilog/VHDL)
  • Low-latency systems & hardware-accelerated execution
  • Technical interview on digital logic, systems, and code
  • Integrates hardware directly into live desk strategies
Article IV · Section 2

Who Can Apply?

Any University of Michigan student — faculty and staff too. There's no graduation-year requirement.

Merit alone decides admission. No member or officer may grant, promise, or guarantee a spot.

Recruiting runs at the start of every semester — applications, then interviews, for both tracks.

No stage of the process can be bypassed — every applicant completes the full application and interviews.
