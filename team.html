Leadership & Structure

The People Behind Every Desk

MTT is governed by an elected board of Directors, three rotating Partners, and desk-level leadership — all held to the same merit-based standard as every member.

Article V · Section 4

The Partners

MTT's three Partners are its Authorized Signers with the University — always the members holding the Director of Trading, Director of Engineering, and Director of Technology seats. Together they share the role equivalent of a President, set MTT's overall direction, and rotate weekly on opening and closing general meetings.

Article V · Section 1

The Board of Directors

Five elected officers govern MTT's trading, research, engineering, technology, and logistics operations. Hover — or tap on mobile — to flip each card.

01 / BOARD

Director of Trading

Risk, capital allocation & live desk performance.

Leads evaluation and approval of new desks, sets the research and testing standards a strategy must meet before trading, and reviews existing desks' strategies over time.

Oversees MTT's hardware and software strategies — FPGA and low-latency systems, hardware-accelerated trading projects, equipment, and hardware-focused industry partnerships.

Director of Technology

Platforms, data pipelines & infrastructure.

Oversees software and technical infrastructure — trading platforms, data pipelines, backtesting tools — and integrates hardware with MTT's trading systems and accounts.

ELECTED ANNUALLY

Article VI · Section 5

Project Desks

Below the board, members are organized into desks — small teams that carry a trading or hardware project from research through live execution.

H

Desk Head

Directs the desk's projects and reports progress to the board every week. Elected by desk members or appointed by the board.

T

Desk Trader

Trading-track members who research, test, and execute strategy within the desk.

E

Desk Engineer

Hardware-track members building the FPGA and low-latency systems the desk depends on.

By the Rules

Desk Limits

  • No more than one desk per three active members, including board members.
  • Every desk needs at least three members, with at least one on each track.
  • Desks may only be fully dissolved by a unanimous board vote.
  • Members move between desks only between semesters.
