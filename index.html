Our Mission
MET exists to train students at the intersection of electronic trading and hardware engineering — so members can learn, build, and deploy across both disciplines without boundaries.

— Article III, MET Constitution

We operate through project desks: small teams of trading, hardware, and software members who design, build, and run real trading systems — from strategy research to low-latency execution on FPGAs and other hardware.

New members also complete a weekly education program designed to build technical skills early in their college careers, so they leave ready for careers at top trading firms, hardware and systems companies, or their own ventures.

How Desks Operate

From Proposal to Live Execution

Every project desk moves through a defined lifecycle, with risk limits and board oversight at each stage.

01

Research Phase

Approved desks develop and test their trading strategy or hardware project. No live trading occurs until a working system or validated edge is demonstrated.

02

Live Execution

Desks are approved for live trading by the Director of Trading, with written risk limits, capital allocation, and weekly performance review.

03

Continuous Oversight

A desk that breaks its risk limits or stops producing results returns to the Research Phase until approved to resume.

Two Tracks, One Desk

Choose Your Track

Every member joins through the Trading or Hardware track, and works side-by-side with the other on every desk.

Trading Track

Strategy & Execution

Research market edges, build and backtest strategies, and run live risk on desk capital.

  • Strategy research & backtesting
  • Risk management & position limits
  • Live execution under board-approved limits
Hardware Track

FPGAs & Low-Latency Systems

Design and build the hardware-accelerated systems that get orders to the exchange faster.

  • FPGA & RTL development
  • Low-latency systems engineering
  • Hardware integration into desk strategies
